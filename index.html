Meet Caryn

One woman, several chapters — bound by a single belief in the power of telling your story.

Caryn Walker is a Sunday Times best-selling author, a qualified life coach, and the founder of Britannia School of Language. Her autobiography, Tell Me You're Sorry, Daddy, tells how she brought her father to justice decades after he abused her throughout her childhood — written to give a voice to her late sister, Jenny, and to everyone who needs their story heard.

Today she pours that same belief into her work: helping people find confidence and resilience through coaching, and opening doors to the world through language.
