The approach

CBT and Person-Centred, in whatever mix helps you.

Caryn draws on two complementary approaches, adapted to what you need session to session.

01

Person-Centred Counselling

A warm, non-directive space where you lead. Caryn listens without judgement, helping you find your own clarity and pace.

02

Cognitive Behavioural Therapy

Practical, structured tools to understand the link between thoughts, feelings and behaviour — and to make real, lasting change.

03

Trauma-informed care

Every session is paced around safety and trust — never pushing further or faster than feels right for you.

04

Lived understanding

Caryn brings her own experience of survival to this work, alongside her professional training.
